# Erthi public identity and contributor privacy

Erthi | إرثي (https://erthi.org) is the public identity of an individually operated free/open-source Islamic software initiative. The operator's legal name is not published in project-facing materials.

- Contact: admin@erthi.org
- Repository management by Erthi-Org does not constitute independent legal personhood.
- Protect private ownership documents, addresses, personal emails, credentials and signing keys.
- Use project-facing credits, but retain legally required third-party attribution and author/license notices.
- Do not rewrite existing Git history merely to hide contributors.
- Apache-2.0 covers the repository's software code, not third-party Quranic text, hadith collections, translations, recitations, imagery or fonts.
- Publish religious content only with verified provenance, rights and appropriate scholarly review.
- Multilingual content must distinguish translations of meaning from original Arabic Quran text.
